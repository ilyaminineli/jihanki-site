# Japan — place references and photographic identifications

This register preserves named stops established during the journey. A few early references do not carry enough context to identify a precise destination; those remain unresolved rather than being assigned guessed names or coordinates.

## Confirmed named stops

Iyashi no Sato, Ryugu Cave, Kitaguchi Hongu Fuji Sengen Shrine, Fujisan Museum, Oishi Park and Lake Kawaguchi, Katori Jingu and Katori Station.

## Identification policy

Photographs are assigned to a place only when a visible sign, recognisable landmark, traveller confirmation or reliable place reference supports the identification. Regional context and thematic similarities are not treated as exact locations. Map positions remain schematic, not navigation coordinates.
