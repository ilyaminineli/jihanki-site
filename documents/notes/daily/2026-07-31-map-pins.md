## Map policy

The Memory Map now separates **PLACE**, **MEMORY**, **TRAILS** and **PINS**. The PINS view preserves the original short links and labels each pin according to the evidence present in the export. Caption translations are not presented as official place names. Unlabelled pins do not receive invented coordinates or map positions.

All map positions remain schematic and are not navigation coordinates.
