## Later imaginative reading — the tunnel that remembers (6 August)

On 6 August, Julia developed the tunnel idea into a more specific piece of speculative fiction. In her imagined version, the tunnel acts as a recording device: footsteps, coins, engines and bicycle movement accumulate as traces in its walls. Each sound seems to belong to a different layer of the area's past.

The fiction gives the tunnel a nightly **archive rotation**: older traces are overwritten, while new sounds enter the memory. A doubled image on a screen becomes two moments occupying the same frame; a camera-shutter sound suggests that the tunnel has recorded the passer-by in return. The tunnel is personified as an artist that wants to be noticed.

The imagined mica-and-vibration mechanism belongs to the artwork's fictional logic. It is not a factual claim about the real tunnel's construction, materials or recording technology. This later interpretation is part of Julia Baranyuk / Takismana's creative development of the field encounter, not a literal explanation of the place.
