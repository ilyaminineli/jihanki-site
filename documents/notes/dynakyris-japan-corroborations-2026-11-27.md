# DYNAKYRIS — Japan fieldnote corroborations

**Source:** the supplied DYNAKYRIS field-notes JSON. This is a companion account, not a replacement for the travellers' own chronology.

## Kyoto — Banbutsu Sōzō-bō and physical work

DYNAKYRIS's 13 August 2026 fieldnote describes the Kyoto leg and a visit to **万物創造房 (Banbutsu Sōzō-bō)**. He recalls the shop's unusual mix of retail and domestic space and compares it with an earlier visit of his own. This strengthens the existing identification of `photo-22` as the Banbutsu Sōzō-bō interior, while remaining his separately attributed recollection.

The same entry describes prints and artwork previously shown in Moscow being carried through Japan and shared or left behind. It also describes a black Nintendo DS Lite and Japanese games, including **Daigasso! Band Brothers DX**. These details add a physical-art exchange dimension to the Kyoto shop memory. The JSON does not establish the exact date of the Banbutsu visit.

## Local celebration — candidate connection

In a 16 August entry, DYNAKYRIS describes a local celebration from the previous evening with fireworks, dancing, local people, an invitation, conversation and hospitality. He notes a shop with a house attached and compares the arrangement to Banbutsu Sōzō-bō.

The description is compatible with the Lake Kawaguchi / Nagahama area. The town identifies Nagahama as the western side of Lake Kawaguchi and says its Furusato Festival takes place each 15 August ([Fujikawaguchiko Town](https://www.town.fujikawaguchiko.lg.jp/ka/info.php?if_id=740)). A 2026 event listing gives fireworks at Nagahama Shinsui Park on 15 August ([event listing](https://www.walkerplus.com/event/ar0419e195711/data.html)). This is a plausible match for photos `addition_4 (7).jpg` and `addition_4 (8).jpg`, but neither the note nor images names the venue. The event remains provisional.

Photo `addition_4 (11).jpg` identifies **Kadoya Shoten**, which the traveller says belongs to the man they contacted in Saiko and who introduced himself as `山梨県河口湖のかどや商店の三浦正重でさ`. This is kept as a separate contact memory in [Kadoya Shoten — Miura contact](./contacts/kadoya-shoten-miura.md). DYNAKYRIS does not name Kadoya Shoten, so it is not assumed to be the shop-with-a-house he described.

## The Mountain Remembers — memory and collaboration

DYNAKYRIS's 17 August notes describe `The Mountain Remembers` as a work of memory rather than a travelogue. He associates it with Fuji Five Lakes, Saiko, Aokigahara, roads, shrines, tunnels, vending machines and field recordings, and describes landscapes becoming characters in recollection.

He names **Akane Iirai, Eugene and Takismana** as the three UTAU voices. He also records **DYNAKYRIS in Katakana** in the credits. His account describes source imagery, redrawing, frame-by-frame Photoshop animation, engraving-like treatment and analog distortion. These are the collaborator's observations, not a replacement for the project's own production documentation.

All material here is attributed to DYNAKYRIS, not written as Ilya or Julia's first-person memory. No unverified addresses, event names or shop identities are assigned.
