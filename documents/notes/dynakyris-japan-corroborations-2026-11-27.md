# DYNAKYRIS — selected Japan diary extracts

The entries below preserve DYNAKYRIS’s perspective alongside the main journey chronology. They are edited English summaries of diary entries, not verbatim quotations.

## 13 August 2026 — Kyoto / Banbutsu Sōzō-bō

DYNAKYRIS describes visiting **万物創造房 (Banbutsu Sōzō-bō)**, the Kyoto shop run by **Banbutsu-san**. He recalls its unusual combination of retail and domestic space and compares it with an earlier visit of his own.

The same Kyoto entry describes prints and artwork previously shown in Moscow being carried through Japan and shared or left behind. It also mentions a black Nintendo DS Lite and Japanese game software, including **Daigasso! Band Brothers DX**.

The archive’s photograph **photo-22** records the Banbutsu Sōzō-bō shop interior. This diary entry preserves DYNAKYRIS’s personal account of the visit and his connection with Banbutsu-san.

## 17 August 2026 — The Mountain Remembers

DYNAKYRIS describes *The Mountain Remembers* as a work of memory rather than a travelogue. He associates it with Fuji Five Lakes, Saiko, Aokigahara, roads, shrines, tunnels, vending machines and field recordings, and describes landscapes becoming characters in recollection.

He names **Akane Iirai, Eugene and Takismana** among the three UTAU voices and notes that DYNAKYRIS appears in Katakana in the credits. His account also describes source imagery, redrawing, frame-by-frame Photoshop animation, engraving-like treatment and analog distortion.
