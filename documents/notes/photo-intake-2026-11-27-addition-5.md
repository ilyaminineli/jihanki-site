## How this should connect to the existing archive

1. **Uno-Misaki is a distinct named tunnel.** The `(49)` portal sign matches the 25 July daily chapter, which records the first encounter, photographs and field recordings at Uno-Misaki Tunnel. The tunnel belongs with the `tunnel` project and that field chapter. The interior in `(4)` is a candidate same-site frame, not yet confirmed. Do not confuse either with Niikura Tunnel (`photo-18`, `photo-181`, `photo-182`).
2. **Miyuki Beach cross-reference.** The `(48)` wooden sign is a strong addition to the existing Miyuki Beach locality already used for `photo-202`. It may be linked as nearby locality evidence, but it does not identify the school in `photo-202`.
3. **Kawaguchiko toy museum candidate.** The sequence `(8)`–`(14)` expands the archive’s existing 26 July account of simple electronic toys, Famicom-like play, rows of monster figures and nostalgic toys. The venue needs confirmation; do not confuse it with the Kyoto friend’s shop Banbutsu Sōzō-bō (`photo-22`).
4. **Lake Yamanaka / Lake Shōji geography.** The sign and navigation screen establish these as named regional references in the supplied collection. They justify adding place anchors and within-Yamanashi relationships, but not drawing a specific route or asserting the whole forest sequence was shot beside one lake.
5. **Deer and Shiki.** The photographs may enrich the 24–25 July Shiki encounter memory, but the animal identity and sequence need the traveller’s confirmation.
6. **The new images do not resolve the Satyam 1 story.** No image in this batch supplies enough evidence to merge the memorial/boundary landscape with the traveller-confirmed Satyam 1 clearing from `addition_4 (1).jpg`. Keep it separate unless confirmed from memory.
7. **Okinawa should stay separate from the Fuji/Kansai regional road trail.** The Miyuki Beach sign is a local cross-reference to the existing Okinawa image, not evidence that it belongs to the August Kawaguchiko–Kyoto–Osaka road loop.
